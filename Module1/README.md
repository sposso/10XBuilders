## Objectives

1. Identify whether we are using AI models with **automation complacency**.  
   Automation complacency implies over-reliance on automated systems, leading to reduced vigilance, critical thinking, and oversight.

   It can cause several problems, such as:
   - **Automation bias**: the tendency to favor suggestions from automated systems, even when they are wrong.
   - **Skill atrophy**: the loss of expertise due to reduced active engagement.

2. Avoid using AI models with automation complacency by applying **hybrid engineering**.  
   In this workflow, the human leads and the AI implements.

   To execute this workflow, a structured loop named **BPIR** is proposed.

## BPIR Framework

BPIR is composed of four stages that should be followed when working with AI:

- **B — Brief**: The human defines the context, restrictions, and definition of done.
- **P — Plan**: The AI model proposes a plan, and the human validates it.
- **I — Implementation**: The AI model generates code iteratively.
- **R — Review**: The human audits the logic, security, and alignment with the initial plan.
